Q&A / Troubleshooting

Common closed alpha questions for testers and families.

I cannot access the test. What should I do?

Make sure you are using the Google account that was added to the tester list. If you are unsure, contact the developer and provide the email address connected to your Google Play account.

The install button is missing. What should I do?

Join the test from the invite link first. Then reopen the Play Store link on your Android device. It may take a few minutes for Google Play access to update.

Does Lightning Pong require an internet connection?

Lightning Pong v0.2 for Android is designed to play offline. Google Play may require internet access to download, install, or update the app. The planned browser demo requires a connection, and its optional account features use online services.

Does the game collect personal information?

The v0.2 Android game does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information. Players age 13 or older may optionally create a browser account when that feature becomes available. See the Privacy Policy for the information used by those optional services.

Will I need an account for the browser demo?

No. The three-level browser demo is planned to remain playable without an account. An optional account can add cloud saves, alpha enrollment, and reviewed score submissions.

Are my submitted scores public?

Not automatically. Publication requires a chosen handle, leaderboard opt-in, permission for that run, and acceptance through the score-review process.

Why did my progress disappear?

Progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may delete saved progress.

What should I test first?

Start with Level 1, then Level 2 if it unlocks. Check controls, sound, progress saving, victory and defeat screens, and whether menus are easy to understand.

Can children test the game?

Only testers approved by the developer should participate in the closed test. Self-managed accounts require age 13 or older. Parents or guardians should supervise younger testers and send feedback through the approved adult contact.
