Before a profile is created

Clear choices first.

Self-managed profiles will be limited to players age 13 or older. The game demo will not require an account, and joining a leaderboard will remain a separate choice.

Private by default

Email, saves, enrollment status, and unapproved score claims stay private. Public leaderboard entries use a chosen display name rather than an email address.

Your exit stays visible

The finished account page will begin export and deletion requests, with the published privacy contact available as a fallback.

History with judgment

Turning off leaderboard consent will normally stop future publication while leaving approved historical records intact. Removal remains available when needed.
