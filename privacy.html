Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Applies to: Lightning Pong v0.2 Android Closed Alpha, browser services, and the Flash Campaign

This Privacy Policy explains how Lightning Pong handles information in the offline Android game and in optional browser account features.

Lightning Pong v0.2 is an offline arcade game distributed for closed testing through Google Play. A separate three-level browser demo is planned to remain playable without an account. Players age 13 or older may optionally use account features when they become available.

Android Game Data

The Lightning Pong v0.2 Android game does not require a player account and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information.

The Android game does not currently collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal files
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Health or fitness information
  • Financial information
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes
  • Player analytics

Lightning Pong v0.2 does not contain advertising or developer-operated analytics.

Local Game Data

Lightning Pong stores information locally on your device so that the game can function and remember your progress.

This local information may include:

  • Game progress and cleared levels
  • Player progression and PX
  • Unlocks and achievements
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Cosmetic and gameplay selections
  • Other information necessary to maintain your local game state

This game data remains locally on the player's device and is not intentionally transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

Lightning Pong v0.2 disables Android application backup for this locally stored game data.

Deleting the application or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored progress and settings.

Internet Access and Online Services

Lightning Pong v0.2 is designed to operate offline.

The application does not request Android Internet permission and does not use developer-operated online services for gameplay, accounts, analytics, advertising, or cloud storage.

Google Play and the Android platform may separately process information associated with downloading, installing, updating, securing, or distributing the application. Such platform-level processing is governed by the applicable platform provider's own terms and privacy practices and is not information intentionally collected by Lightning Pong.

Optional Browser Accounts

The planned three-level browser demo will remain playable without an account. Players age 13 or older may optionally create an account using an email magic link or Google sign-in.

If you choose to use account features, Lightning Pong may process:

  • Your email address and authentication record
  • An optional private display name and chosen game handle
  • Alpha enrollment choices, status, and consent records
  • Up to three browser-demo cloud-save slots
  • Submitted run details, including build, ruleset, completion time, Px used, and limited run evidence
  • Security and service logs needed to operate and protect the account service

These optional services are planned to use Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Supabase processes this information on behalf of the developer. The public website and browser demo are hosted through GitHub Pages.

Leaderboards

Leaderboard publication is a separate choice. A score is not published unless the player has chosen a valid handle, enabled leaderboard participation, marked the run for publication, and the run has been accepted through a trusted review process.

A published entry may show the approved handle, score category, build and ruleset, completion time, Px used, and publication date. It does not show the player's email address, authentication ID, legal name, or private save data.

Disabling future leaderboard participation does not remove historical entries automatically. Keeping accepted historical entries is the default. A player may separately request removal of prior entries, including materially incorrect or compromised records.

Flash Campaign

Players who choose to enter the Flash Campaign use a Player Passport. The service records rules acceptance, age category and—when applicable—confirmation of parent or legal-guardian permission. It also stores server-issued run identifiers, completion time, PX used, limited run evidence, eligibility status, and review decisions.

Developer, QA, and automated research accounts are marked as excluded from prizes. Their runs may be retained privately for game research but are not placed on public prize tables.

A TikTok or Douyin promotional entry includes the submitted video link, platform username, account email, submission time, rights confirmation, and optional repost permission. Entries and contact details remain private to the participant and authorized review team unless publication or reposting is separately permitted.

Advertising and Analytics

Lightning Pong v0.2 does not display advertisements.

The game does not use developer-operated player analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Lightning Pong does not require players to provide personal information in order to play.

Self-managed Lightning Pong accounts are limited to users age 13 or older. Testing involving a younger player must be managed through an authorized adult unless a separate verified parental-consent process is introduced in the future.

Sharing or Selling Personal Information

Lightning Pong does not sell player personal information.

Lightning Pong does not share player personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Optional account information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase and GitHub only as needed to provide, secure, and host the applicable services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress, PX, settings, unlocks, and related game-state information are stored locally on the player's device.

Players may remove Android game information by clearing Lightning Pong's application data or uninstalling the application. The developer cannot independently retrieve that offline local data.

For optional browser accounts:

  • Active profile and cloud-save data is retained while the account remains active or until it is deleted.
  • Withdrawn or declined alpha requests are retained for up to 12 months, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Rejected score submissions are retained for up to 90 days, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Accepted leaderboard entries remain as historical records by default, subject to a separate removal request.
  • Deleted-account information is removed or de-identified from active systems within 30 days. Residual copies in provider-managed backups expire under the provider's normal backup cycle.

Account export and deletion requests will begin from the account page when accounts are enabled. The contact method on this site remains available as a fallback.

Data Security

Lightning Pong v0.2 minimizes Android data exposure by keeping game-state information locally on the player's device. Optional browser services use access controls intended to keep profiles, saves, enrollment records, and unapproved score claims private.

No method of electronic storage or software operation can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Lightning Pong is under active development.

If a future version changes how player information is accessed, collected, used, stored, transmitted, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

The effective date at the top of this policy will identify the current version.
