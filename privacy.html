Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Applies to: Lightning Pong v0.2 Closed Alpha

This Privacy Policy explains how Lightning Pong handles information when you install and play the Android version of the game.

Lightning Pong v0.2 is an offline arcade game currently being distributed for closed testing through Google Play.

Information We Collect

Lightning Pong does not require a player account and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information.

The game does not currently collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal files
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Health or fitness information
  • Financial information
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes
  • Player analytics

Lightning Pong v0.2 does not contain advertising or developer-operated analytics.

Local Game Data

Lightning Pong stores information locally on your device so that the game can function and remember your progress.

This local information may include:

  • Game progress and cleared levels
  • Player progression and PX
  • Unlocks and achievements
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Cosmetic and gameplay selections
  • Other information necessary to maintain your local game state

This game data remains locally on the player's device and is not intentionally transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

Lightning Pong v0.2 disables Android application backup for this locally stored game data.

Deleting the application or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored progress and settings.

Internet Access and Online Services

Lightning Pong v0.2 is designed to operate offline.

The application does not request Android Internet permission and does not use developer-operated online services for gameplay, accounts, analytics, advertising, or cloud storage.

Google Play and the Android platform may separately process information associated with downloading, installing, updating, securing, or distributing the application. Such platform-level processing is governed by the applicable platform provider's own terms and privacy practices and is not information intentionally collected by Lightning Pong.

Advertising and Analytics

Lightning Pong v0.2 does not display advertisements.

The game does not use developer-operated player analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Lightning Pong does not require players to provide personal information in order to play.

The game does not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children through the application.

If the game's data practices or intended audience change in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

Sharing or Selling Personal Information

Lightning Pong does not sell player personal information.

Because Lightning Pong v0.2 does not intentionally collect player personal information, the developer does not intentionally share player personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress, PX, settings, unlocks, and related game-state information are stored locally on the player's device.

The developer does not maintain a server-side player account or database containing this local game information.

Players may remove locally stored game information by clearing Lightning Pong's application data or uninstalling the application. Because the developer does not receive or maintain this local game data, the developer cannot independently retrieve or delete it from a remote server.

Data Security

Lightning Pong v0.2 minimizes data exposure by keeping game-state information locally on the player's device and by not intentionally transmitting that information to developer-operated servers.

No method of electronic storage or software operation can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Lightning Pong is under active development.

If a future version changes how player information is accessed, collected, used, stored, transmitted, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

The effective date at the top of this policy will identify the current version.
