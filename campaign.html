Quick official rules · version 2026-11-13-v1

Fair play, plain language.

Eligibility

Worldwide where lawful. Adults may enter directly. Players age 13 through their local age of majority need verifiable parent or legal-guardian permission. Void where prohibited or lawful prize delivery is unavailable.

No purchase

No purchase is necessary and paying does not improve the chance of winning. This is a skill-based promotional contest.

Ties

If a verified result remains exactly tied and cannot be reliably ordered, the applicable prize is divided equally among the tied eligible participants.

Payment

Prizes are stated in USD. Cash App is preferred, followed by Venmo or a mutually acceptable lawful transfer or region-compatible electronic gift card.

Timing

Early clear prizes are normally sent 24–72 hours after verification. Remaining awards are sent within seven days after the event closes, no later than November 29, 2026, when winner contact and lawful fulfillment are available.

Original work

Promo entrants retain ownership and confirm they made the entry or have permission to submit it. Viewing and judging are required; reposting is optional and requires separate permission in the Passport.
