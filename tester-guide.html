Closed Alpha Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test Lightning Pong. This page explains how to join the test, what to try first, and how to report anything that breaks or feels confusing.

Step 1 - Join the Google Play Test

Use the invite link shared by the developer. You may need to sign in with the same Google account you use on your Android device.

If Google Play says you do not have access, contact the developer and confirm which Google account should be added to the tester list.

Step 2 - Install Lightning Pong

After joining the test, install Lightning Pong from Google Play.

If the install button does not appear right away, wait a few minutes, close and reopen Google Play, or try opening the invite link again on the Android device.

Step 3 - First Test Pass

  1. Open the game.
  2. Start Level 1.
  3. Play until you win or lose.
  4. Return to the level screen.
  5. Confirm progress, PX, or unlocks appear to save.
  6. Try Level 2 if it unlocks.
  7. Check whether the music and sound behave normally.

What To Report

Please report anything that blocks play, looks broken, sounds wrong, or feels confusing.

Helpful reports include:

  • Your device model.
  • Android version if you know it.
  • What level you were playing.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Whether it happened once or repeatedly.
  • A screenshot or short video if possible.

High Priority Bugs

  • App will not install.
  • App crashes or closes.
  • Screen appears blank.
  • Controls do not respond.
  • Sound does not play.
  • Progress disappears unexpectedly.
  • You cannot start or finish a level.
  • A button or menu cannot be read or tapped.

Tester Mindset

You do not need to be a technical expert. The most useful feedback is often simple: what you tried, what you saw, and where you got stuck.

Android Saves and Future Browser Accounts

The current Android alpha keeps progress locally on the Android device. A future optional browser account may provide separate cloud saves, alpha enrollment, and reviewed score submissions for the three-level browser demo. Creating a browser account will not be required to play that demo.

Self-managed accounts will require age 13 or older. See the Privacy Policy before choosing any optional account feature.
